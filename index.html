I’m a second-year M.S. student at the Multimodal Language Processing Lab, KAIST, advised by Prof. KyungTae Lim. I received my B.S. in Computer Science & Engineering and Mathematical Sciences at Seoul National University.

I work on the alignment and interpretability of multimodal language models, with an interest in AI safety. I enjoy working on cases where apparent correctness conceals a failure. An answer may not be grounded in what the model actually observed, and a harm may survive filtering because it reads as benign. I look for these failures, identify what is going wrong and why it goes unnoticed, then explore ways to address them.
